BIOBUZZ scoring quick reference

Key details

  • Each HIVE TIP lets the alliance enter one staged NECTAR; with 60 s left, all remaining NECTAR may be entered.
  • FLOWERS can't be scored until 1:00 remains. Elements go in from the top, between the top and middle rings.
  • A FLOWER is owned by the alliance with the top-most qualifying NECTAR of its color — the owner gets 2 pts for every element in it, no matter who placed them.
  • Tips finished before TELEOP count as AUTO.
